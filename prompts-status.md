## Maintenance

- **Add a prompt:** create the `.md` in `ROVO Prompts/`, add the entry to the `MANIFEST` in `build-site.py` (category, title, blurb, tags), re-run `python3 build-site.py`, update this table.
- **Edit a prompt:** edit the `.md`, re-run `build-site.py`. The copyable body is everything after the first `---` in the file.
- **Rosters (names/account IDs):** live inside the individual prompts (Rovo has no file access). If the team changes, update the affected prompts and regenerate.
